FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Everything you need to know before you start clipping.

What can I save with ClipNote? +

You can save text clips, notes, webpages, and supported video moments. The exact capabilities depend on the ClipNote extension and platform.

Can I record video? +

ClipNote is designed to support video recording alongside your normal clipping workflow.

Can I search my saved clips? +

Yes. Your saved content can be organized and searched so you can find previous ideas without digging through old tabs.

Do my clips keep their source? +

Clips can retain source information so you can return to the original context.

Is ClipNote free? +

ClipNote's availability and feature limits depend on the current version of the extension.

What browsers are supported? +

Check the extension installation page for the currently supported browsers.
