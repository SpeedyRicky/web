FAQ

The questions people actually ask.

Is ClipRoots free?

Yes. Install the extension and start clipping — but you do have to have a account to clip but all you do is put your email make a username and a password

Where do my clips go?

Every clip gets its own page with a shareable link, plus a listing in the public feed if you choose to share it there.

Can I clip a video, not just text?

Yes — mark an in and out point on any supported video and ClipRoots saves the timestamped moment, not just a screenshot.

What if someone clips my content without permission?

Use the "Report a problem" button on any clip page. See our DMCA policy for the full process.
