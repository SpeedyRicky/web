1. Overview

ClipRoots helps you save, annotate, organize, and revisit useful information from the web.

We designed ClipRoots to make your data practices understandable. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use ClipRoots and how that information is used.

In simple terms ClipRoots only uses information that is necessary to provide the features you choose to use.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, ClipRoots may process the following information:

InformationWhy it may be needed
Account informationTo authenticate users and provide account-related functionality.
Notes and annotationsTo save and display the content you create.
Selected webpage textTo create the clip or annotation you explicitly request.
Webpage URL and titleTo identify and link your saved content back to its source.
AI-generated contentTo provide AI features when you explicitly request them.

3. How We Use Information

Information handled by ClipRoots may be used to:

  • Provide note-taking and annotation features.
  • Save and display your clips and notes.
  • Connect saved notes with their source webpages.
  • Provide account functionality where applicable.
  • Generate AI features when you request them.
  • Maintain and improve the reliability of ClipRoots.

We do not use your information for purposes unrelated to providing or improving the service unless we explain that use and, where required, obtain your permission.

4. Webpage Access

ClipRoots may access information from a webpage when you actively use a feature that requires it, such as creating a clip or annotation.

This can include information such as selected text, the webpage title, and the webpage URL.

ClipRoots does not intentionally collect your general browsing history simply because you visit websites.

5. Data Sharing

ClipRoots does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate specific ClipRoots features, such as authentication, storage, hosting, or AI functionality.

When third-party services are used, information is shared only as necessary for the relevant feature and subject to the applicable service's terms and privacy practices.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by ClipRoots against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

Depending on the ClipRoots features you use, you may be able to:

  • Delete notes or annotations you created.
  • Remove saved clips.
  • Stop using optional AI features.
  • Remove or disable the ClipRoots extension.
  • Contact us with questions about your information.

8. Cookies, Local Storage & Your Privacy Rights

ClipRoots does not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. The following is stored locally in your browser or through our authentication provider:

  • Theme preference — saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers your light/dark/auto choice.
  • Sign-in session — when you sign in to comment, our authentication provider (Supabase) stores a session token in your browser so you stay signed in between visits.

You can clear either of these at any time by clearing your browser's site data for ClipRoots.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your information — for example, the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you under laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK) or PIPEDA (Canada), and, for California residents under the CCPA/CPRA, the right to know what personal information is collected and to opt out of its sale (ClipRoots does not sell personal information). To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in the Contact section below.

9. DMCA Policy

ClipRoots Copyright / DMCA Takedown Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

ClipRoots ("we," "us," "our") respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users of the ClipRoots browser extension and feed (the "Service") to do the same. We respond to notices of alleged copyright infringement that comply with the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act ("DMCA") and other applicable laws.

1. Reporting Claimed Infringement

If you believe that content published through ClipRoots (a "clip") infringes your copyright, you can report it two ways:

  • In-app: use the "File a claim" option on the clip in question, or
  • By email/mail: send a written notice to our Designated Agent, below.

To be effective, a notice must be a written communication that includes substantially the following (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)):

  1. A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  3. Identification of the material you claim is infringing, and information reasonably sufficient to let us locate it (e.g. the clip's URL or the source page it was quoted from).
  4. Your contact information: address, telephone number, and email address.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the above information is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.

2. Designated Agent

Notices of claimed infringement should be sent to:

3. Our Process

Upon receiving a notice that substantially complies with the requirements above, we will:

  1. Remove or disable access to the identified clip, typically within 1-2 business days.
  2. Notify the user who published the clip that it was removed, and forward a copy of the notice to them.
  3. Document the claim and the action taken.

We reserve the right to remove content that appears to infringe without waiting for a complete notice, at our discretion.

4. Counter-Notification

If you believe your clip was removed in error or as a result of misidentification, you may submit a counter-notice to our Designated Agent containing (17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3)):

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of the material removed and its location before removal.
  3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  4. Your name, address, and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for your district (or, if outside the U.S., any district in which we may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who filed the original notice.

If we receive a valid counter-notice, we may reinstate the content in 10-14 business days unless the original claimant informs us they have filed a court action.

5. Repeat Infringer Policy

ClipRoots will, in appropriate circumstances, disable or terminate the accounts of users who are determined to be repeat infringers. A user who is the subject of three or more claims filed against their clips is subject to automatic review, and their published clips may be removed pending that review — see our Terms of Service for how this works in practice.

6. Misrepresentations

Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), any person who knowingly materially misrepresents that material is infringing, or that it was removed by mistake, may be liable for damages.
