1. Overview

ClipNoter helps you save, annotate, organize, and revisit useful information from the web.

We designed ClipNoter to make your data practices understandable. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use ClipNoter and how that information is used.

In simple terms ClipNoter only uses information that is necessary to provide the features you choose to use.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, ClipNoter may process the following information:

Information Why it may be needed
Account information To authenticate users and provide account-related functionality.
Notes and annotations To save and display the content you create.
Selected webpage text To create the clip or annotation you explicitly request.
Webpage URL and title To identify and link your saved content back to its source.
AI-generated content To provide AI features when you explicitly request them.

3. How We Use Information

Information handled by ClipNoter may be used to:

  • Provide note-taking and annotation features.
  • Save and display your clips and notes.
  • Connect saved notes with their source webpages.
  • Provide account functionality where applicable.
  • Generate AI features when you request them.
  • Maintain and improve the reliability of ClipNoter.

We do not use your information for purposes unrelated to providing or improving the service unless we explain that use and, where required, obtain your permission.

4. Webpage Access

ClipNoter may access information from a webpage when you actively use a feature that requires it, such as creating a clip or annotation.

This can include information such as selected text, the webpage title, and the webpage URL.

ClipNoter does not intentionally collect your general browsing history simply because you visit websites.

5. Data Sharing

ClipNoter does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate specific ClipNoter features, such as authentication, storage, hosting, or AI functionality.

When third-party services are used, information is shared only as necessary for the relevant feature and subject to the applicable service's terms and privacy practices.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by ClipNoter against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

Depending on the ClipNoter features you use, you may be able to:

  • Delete notes or annotations you created.
  • Remove saved clips.
  • Stop using optional AI features.
  • Remove or disable the ClipNoter extension.
  • Contact us with questions about your information.
